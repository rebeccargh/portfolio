ABOUT

Originally from the countryside in the UK, I moved to NYC in 2018 for a job in clinical research. I have been many things in my professional life - from clinical researcher to corporate event planner, medical illustrator, and Disney World Cast Member.

I truly believe that the breadth of my life experiences make for a designer who can think about problems from unique and varied perspectives. That said, I'm many thing's outside of my work life too!

When I'm not at work, I love to cook, figure skate when I can (especially in winter!) and still dabble in making cosplays when I get the chance - catch me at NYCC!
